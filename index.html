OUR PHILOSOPHY

Minimalist by design.
Privacy by default.

We are a research-driven product studio obsessed with three ideas: minimal design, privacy as a baseline, and experience as the ultimate measure of craft. Every line of code, every pixel, every motion — they exist to disappear in service of the user.

  • Local-first data processing — your bytes never leave your device unless you say so.
  • Runtime generative systems that produce organic, non-repeating interactions.
  • End-to-end encryption with zero cloud persistence for sensitive surfaces.
  • Zen-inspired aesthetics: deliberate restraint over decorative excess.

TECHNICAL DEPTH

Programmatic generation. No two sessions the same.

We invest deeply in runtime generative systems. Instead of fixed assets and template screens, our products compose themselves moment-to-moment — yielding experiences that feel organic, varied and endlessly surprising.

  • Audio synthesis pipelines that model timbre, spatialisation and rhythm.
  • Visual generators for typography, motion and procedural textures.
  • Interaction graphs that adapt to user intent and biometric state.
  • Cross-platform native performance budgets under 16 ms per frame.
