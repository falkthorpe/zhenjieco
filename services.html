Services built
around craft & trust.

Six specialist engagements, each laser-focused. Whether you need a complete App Store launch, a generative audio system, or a privacy audit of an existing stack — we have a service line scoped precisely for the work.

01 · Mobile Application Development

Native and cross-platform iOS, iPadOS and Android applications engineered for the Apple App Store and Google Play. End-to-end product development including architecture, UI/UX, App Store metadata, screenshots and submission.

iOS · iPadOS · Android

02 · Programmatic Audio Synthesis

Generative audio engines using procedural models. We build audio systems that compose themselves — for meditation, focus, ambient soundscapes and adaptive music — with deterministic seeding and on-device playback.

AUv3 · DSP · Adaptive

03 · Visual Interaction Design

Immersive interaction systems where every gesture, micro-animation and transition is choreographed with intent. Generative typography, procedural textures, motion scripting — all in service of clarity and ease.

UI · Motion · Generative

04 · Privacy & Security Engineering

Privacy audits, threat modelling and full re-engineering for software that needs to be defensible under GDPR, CCPA and PIPL. We design the on-device data flows first, then add the smallest possible network surface on top.

GDPR · CCPA · PIPL

05 · Local-First Data Solutions

Architectures for software that runs entirely off-cloud. Sync frameworks, conflict resolution, encrypted local stores and opt-in cloud peer relays — for products where the cloud is an optional convenience, not a requirement.

Sync · CRDT · E2EE

06 · Creative Tech Consulting

Hands-on technical advisory for teams pushing creative boundaries. Architecture review, prototype sprints, R&D advisory, whitepaper co-authorship. We embed with your team for 6-12 week engagements.

R&D · Prototype · Advisory
OUR WORKFLOW

A disciplined, transparent six-stage process.

We optimise for clarity. Every engagement begins with a written brief and concludes with a published artefact. The middle is open-source communication.

STAGE 01

Discovery & Brief

A 1–2 week immersion. We read your codebase, audit your market, write a written brief with explicit non-goals.

STAGE 02

Prototype Sprint

A working prototype on device — composable, interactive, tested with five real users — before any commitment.

STAGE 03

Architecture Lock

A frozen technical architecture with documented data flows, encryption boundaries, and rejection of unjustified complexity.

Iterative Build

Bi-weekly TestFlight builds with structured feedback rounds. We instrument the app so every motion is measurable.

STAGE 05

Privacy Audit

An independent review against GDPR, CCPA, COPPA and the Apple App Store privacy labels. Documentation delivered with the build.
